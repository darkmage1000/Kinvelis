# Kinvelis: store listing draft

A first draft for the Google Play listing. Every claim below is true of the current build; anything that changes
(price, size, kin count) should be checked again before this is pasted into the Play Console.

## Title (30 characters max)

Kinvelis: Catch, Bond, Build

## Short description (80 characters max)

Catch 200+ kin, build a camp they run, explore 8 lands. No ads, no gacha.

## Full description (4,000 characters max)

Washed ashore on Rimewild Isle with nothing but one small partner, you gather, craft and catch your way across
eight lands to the Wardens who hold a storm shut.

NO TWO KIN ARE ALIKE
Every kin you meet has its own size, build, coat and markings, and an egg takes after both its parents. Catch
206 kinds, from a sprout that naps in sunbeams to a samurai beetle and a witch cat on a broom, then raise the
ones that are yours and nobody else's.

DREAM UP YOUR OWN
Pick a body, ears, horns, a tail, wings and colours, give it a name, and it walks out into the world as a real
kin that fights, works and grows beside you.

A CAMP THAT LIVES
Put kin to work logging, farming, cooking and smithing. They greet you when you come home, nap in the afternoon,
play tag when the work is done and sulk if you forget to feed them. Research new metals, automate the lot, and
it all keeps going while the game is closed.

A BIG WORLD TO WALK
Eight lands, each with its own weather, nights and dangers. Caves, ruins, shipwrecks, relics on mountain
ledges, rival tamers, fishing, legend hunts and ten boss fights. Ride, glide, grapple and surf to get there.

MADE FOR PHONES, AND FOR PEOPLE
- Plays offline. No account, no sign-in.
- No ads. No gacha. No timers to pay past.
- A small download.
- A "Little Explorer" world for young children: nothing to read, nothing to lose, picture goals and a sticker book.
- A "Toy Box" of live switches for players who like to fiddle: kin size, jump height, weather, time of day.

YOUR EXPLORER, YOUR PHOTOS
Make your own explorer, take pictures in photo mode, and save a card for any kin you are proud of.

## Feature bullets (for the graphic or the top of the page)

- 206 kin, each one of a kind
- Design your own kin
- A camp your kin run, and live in
- 8 lands, 10 bosses, a full story
- Offline, no ads, no gacha
- A mode small children can play

## Words to lean on, and words to leave alone

Use our own: kin, Rimewild, Wardens, camp, bond. Avoid "pal", "Pokemon-like" and "Palworld-like": two official
Palworld mobile games are due, and they will own those searches.
